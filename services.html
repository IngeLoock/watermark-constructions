Our services

Three specialties,one standard.

Insurance repair, aged care and commercial building, all delivered by the same family owned team with the same discipline, communication and care.

01  Insurance Repair

Back to normal, as fast as safely possible.

More than 62,000 properties repaired for Australia's major insurers since 2000. From a single kitchen flood to a cyclone, we manage the claim, the customer and the build under one roof, with 24/7 Make Safe response.

  • 24/7/365 Make Safe response
  • Catastrophe and BAU claims
  • Trusted by major insurers
  • Care for vulnerable customers
Insurance Repair
02  Aged Care

Refurbishment that respects the people who live there.

Refurbishments, facilities management, and community and lifestyle projects, planned around residents and staff. Every team member and subcontractor is police and vaccination checked before they set foot on site.

  • Works in live environments
  • Police and vaccination checked
  • ISO 45001 safety certified
  • Metro and regional Queensland
Aged Care

  • Multi residential and strata
  • Government and education
