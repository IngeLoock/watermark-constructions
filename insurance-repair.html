Common questions

The questions we hear most.

Can't see yours? Call us on 1300 377 792 and the team will talk it through.

Do I pay you or my insurer?

In most cases your insurer manages the claim and pays for the approved repairs, and you pay your policy excess. We work with your insurer throughout, so you are not chasing two parties. Your claim contact can confirm the details of your policy.

How long does the assessment take?

It depends on the type of damage and, after a major weather event, how many properties are affected at once. We make safe first, then assess, and your project team will give you a realistic timeframe as soon as the scope is known. After Tropical Cyclone Debbie, our crews were on the ground within 48 hours.

Can I choose my own materials?

Repairs are scoped with your insurer to bring the property back to its condition before the damage, and to current building standards where required. If there are options, your project team will talk them through with you. Anything beyond the approved scope needs to be agreed with your insurer first.

What is a Make Safe?

It is the urgent first response that stops damage getting worse and keeps people safe, such as securing a damaged roof, weatherproofing openings or isolating hazards. It happens before the full repairs are scoped.

Who do I call after hours?

Call 1300 377 792. Our Make Safe response runs 24/7, 365 days a year.

Will you deal with my strata manager or landlord?

Where it applies, yes. We are used to managing multiple stakeholders on a single job, including owners, strata and property managers, and your project administrator will keep everyone updated.
