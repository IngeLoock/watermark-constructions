Our capability

Craftsmanship that respects the people in the room.

We undertake refurbishment and facilities management services throughout Queensland, focusing on both metropolitan and regional areas to provide a reach of support that exceeds our competitors. Our care and craftsmanship across this sector affords an excellence in quality and customer outcome.

Working with not for profit, community and aged care organisations, we are acutely aware of the live working environment, individual sensibilities and the unique considerations required of our aged customers. Every safety aspect of our works is thoroughly implemented and supervised, with the same high level of respect and consideration extended to all residents and visitors.

What we deliver

Services for aged care operators.

01

Refurbishments

Room, wing and common area refurbishments planned around residents and staff, with staged works that keep facilities operating safely throughout.

02

Facilities management

Programmed and reactive maintenance across your portfolio, with a single point of contact and the reporting, KPI and cost controls operators expect.

03

Community and lifestyle projects

Outdoor spaces, lifestyle amenities and community facilities that improve daily life for residents and the people who care for them.

04

Insurance repairs in care settings

Our insurance repair expertise applied to aged care and differently abled facilities, where speed, safety and sensitivity all matter at once.

Safety first

A workforce you can trust around your most vulnerable.

Background police and vaccination checks are conducted on all team members and subcontractors before site access is granted. We support our clients to ensure our workforce keeps the most vulnerable members of the community safe.

  • Police checked team and subcontractors
  • Vaccination checked before site access
  • ISO 45001 safety management certified
  • Works supervised in live environments
  • Respect for residents, families and staff
  • Infection control aware practices
