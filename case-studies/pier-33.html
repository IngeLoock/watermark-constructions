Pier 33,Mooloolaba 2018.

The transformation of a fixture in the Coast's social scene: an extensive renovation of the former Mooloolaba Yacht Club into a Hamptons inspired dining, retail and events venue.

Constructed
2018
Award
Master Builders, Best Commercial Fit Out
Venue

"The transformation of a fixture in the Coast's social scene, beautifully done by Watermark Constructions."
Sally Smith

The brief

Co-owners Andrew Hohns and John Robertson set about the redesign of what was the Mooloolaba Yacht Club, and in 2018 appointed Watermark Constructions as their builder.

"We'd been aware of the impression Watermark Constructions had been making along the eastern seaboard and jumped at the chance to work with them on this project."

Sixty years of unknowns

Pier 33 presented as an extensive renovation project for the commercial projects team. The 60 year old structure was fraught with unknowns, however the team collaborated closely with the owners and designers to produce an exquisite Hamptons inspired outcome that both showcases the original structure and emulates a level of workmanship that enhances every completed space.

  • Three large function spaces
  • A 200 seat dining area with alfresco dining
  • An expansive lawned area
  • Commercial kitchen, office, cold room and amenities

Four weeks to opening

"Watermark Constructions took charge of the venue and it was all hands to the wheel to strip back and fit out the famous Coast venue. It was a major refurbishment with a project timeline of four weeks ahead of the scheduled opening."

"As a family operation we chose Watermark as our builder because we trusted them to understand our desire to put forward an offering with a bit of soul to it, and a point of difference in the Coast venue market."Pier 33 owners

The result was recognised by Master Builders Queensland as Best Commercial Fit Out, and Pier 33 remains one of the Sunshine Coast's signature waterfront venues.
