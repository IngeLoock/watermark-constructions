Built to reach

Metropolitan and regional, with no gaps in between.

We service both metropolitan and regional areas so every community gets the same level of support, not just the major centres. Our capacity to deploy resources on short notice, to any location in Queensland, has seen us working alongside communities affected by cyclone, flood and hail from Cairns to the Gold Coast.

In every catastrophe event we take part in, we employ local contractors we've worked with for years. It grows our regional footprint, keeps work in the community and means we can resource multiple regions at once without compromising on quality.

Where we've worked

Local knowledge in every region.

Each region has an office, a team and local contractors we have worked with for years. These are some of the events and projects behind that.

Far North Queensland

Cairns

Our Cairns office supports the Far North. The region's cyclone history includes Cyclone Larry in 2006, Cyclone Yasi in 2011 and Cyclone Ita in 2014, all part of Watermark's event record.

North Queensland

Townsville and the Whitsundays

We responded to the Townsville floods in 2019 and, after Tropical Cyclone Debbie in 2017, repaired 1,532 properties across the Whitsundays with crews on the ground within 48 hours.

Central Queensland

Yeppoon and Rockhampton

After the April 2020 Rockhampton hailstorm, we rebuilt more than 1,400 homes and businesses over 13 months. We also responded to Cyclone Marcia in 2015 and the Bundaberg and Maryborough events.

South East Queensland

Sunshine Coast and Brisbane

Our home region. Work here ranges from the 2022 Brisbane floods and repeated hailstorms to Pier 33 at Mooloolaba, which won Best Commercial Fit Out from Master Builders Queensland.

New South Wales

Sydney

Our Sydney office at Bondi Junction supports insurance, commercial and aged care clients across New South Wales. Licence NSW 210323C.

Tasmania

Hobart

Our Hobart office in North Hobart brings the same team approach to Tasmanian clients and properties. Licence TAS CC6012N.
