Our Commitment to Starlight Super Steps 2026

CommunityMay 2026

We are proud to support Starlight Super Steps, a national challenge that helps make hospital a happier place for sick kids and their families.

Every minute of every day a child is admitted to hospital in Australia. For those kids, hospital can be a world of tests, procedures and uncertainty. Starlight Children's Foundation partners with health professionals to bring fun, joy and laughter into that world, because happiness is essential to sick kids' overall health and wellbeing.

Through programs like Captain Starlight, Starlight Express Rooms, Livewire, the Healthier Futures Initiative, Starlight Wishes and Starlight Moments, Starlight now creates more than five million positive experiences for seriously ill children across Australia each year.

Super Steps is Starlight's 10,000 steps a day challenge held throughout May. Individuals and teams commit to walking or running 10,000 steps a day and raising funds to support these vital hospital programs. In 2025 alone Starlight delivered more than 163,000 Starlight Express Room visits, 118 remote community visits by Captain Starlight and 424 Starlight Wishes, impact that is only possible thanks to fundraisers and donors.

Why we're involved

As a business we care about the wellbeing of children, families and communities. Supporting Super Steps is one way we can make a tangible difference beyond our day to day work, by helping sick kids experience moments of fun and normalcy at a time they need it most. Walking, running and fundraising as a team also brings our people together around a shared purpose.

How you can help

We invite our clients, partners, suppliers and community to step up with us:

  • Join our team and take on the 10,000 steps a day challenge
  • Donate to our fundraising page, every contribution, large or small, helps
  • Share our campaign to help spread the word about Super Steps

Together we can help Starlight bring more smiles, laughter and support to sick kids and their families across Australia.
