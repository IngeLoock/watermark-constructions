Master Builders BUSSQ Golf Day

Events2023

This event is a great opportunity to network with other Master Builders members, and to say thank you to our team by providing a break from site and the office.

The beautiful Sunshine Coast is home to our national head office, along with a number of spectacular beaches, national parks and golf courses. Twin Waters Golf Club is where the team is playing today, with both the sunshine and course conditions perfect.

We are playing two teams of four, one representing our National Insurance division and the other our Commercial division. There's some friendly rivalry on the course, vying for the top spot and a number of fun prizes, however the connection between our two teams is as strong as ever.

Because who we are as a business, is who we are as people.
