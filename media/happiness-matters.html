Happiness Matters

Culture2024

At Watermark Constructions we create exceptional outcomes for our customers, our clients and our community. Our team make us strong, and their love of what they do brings peace of mind, comfort, light and laughter to the homes and businesses we work in.

Indicative of our spirit, and something remarkably close to our hearts, is the work of the Starlight Children's Foundation Australia.

Our Managing Director, Hew Williams, has been embraced by the Starlight Foundation as a member of the Queensland Advisory Board. Hew is inspired and motivated by the work of Starlight and continues to lend his heart, spirit and energy to contribute to the happiness of sick kids across Australia.

Starlight programs replace pain, fear and stress with fun, joy and laughter, helping sick kids and their families cope with their toughest challenges. Starlight is the broadest reaching children's charity in Australia, supporting kids of all ages regardless of their illness or injury, and creating much needed moments of happiness.

To learn more about the amazing work of the Starlight Foundation, and how you can be a part of bringing smiles and laughter to sick kids, visit starlight.org.au.

Starlight exists because happiness matters.
